Frequently Asked Questions

Does converting PDF to JPG reduce quality?
JPG uses lossy compression. Increase the quality slider for more detail and larger files, or choose PNG for lossless output. The page render size is fixed; this tool does not offer a DPI setting.
Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?
No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your PDFs never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.
Should I choose JPG or PNG?
JPG is best for photo-rich or colorful pages with smaller file sizes. PNG is ideal for text-heavy pages or when transparency is needed, with lossless quality but larger files.
How many pages can I convert at once?
There is no fixed page-count cap, but PDFs must be 100 MB or smaller. More pages use more memory and take longer; split a large document if your browser runs out of memory.
Can I download all pages at once?
Yes. After conversion, you can download individual pages or click "Download All" to get all images packaged in a ZIP file.
